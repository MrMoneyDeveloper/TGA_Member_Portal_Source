# TGA Initial Product Backlog

## Must have for MVP

- Member registration and email verification
- Administrator login with MFA
- Member profile and configurable membership types
- Application checklist and status timeline
- Secure document uploads and review queue
- Configurable online assessments and results
- PayFast sandbox checkout and ITN processing
- Membership activation and renewal dates
- Email notifications
- Searchable member register
- Audit history and basic exports
- Privacy notice, consent, retention, and access controls

## Should have

- Consulting request workflow
- Saved communication templates
- Automated document-expiry and renewal reminders
- Payment reconciliation dashboard
- Support chatbot based on approved content
- Dashboard filters and scheduled reports
- Bulk member import with validation

## Could have

- PWA installation
- QR-verifiable digital membership card
- Mobile document capture
- SMS/WhatsApp notifications
- External CRM/accounting integrations
- Advanced analytics and anomaly detection
