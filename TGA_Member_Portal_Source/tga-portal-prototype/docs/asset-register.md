# TGA prototype asset register

Generated with the built-in image-generation workflow on 21 July 2026. These are approval-stage prototype assets, not documentary photography of TGA people, facilities or events.

## Shared constraints

Every photography prompt required a lawful, non-violent civilian association context; realistic South African adults and environments; calm safety-led handling; charcoal, olive, steel and brass tones; no text, logos or watermark. Prompts excluded children, muzzle flashes, tactical posturing, political material, official-looking uniforms, unsafe trigger position, crosshairs on people, visible personal data and ecommerce styling.

Final public images are center-cropped to 3:2 and supplied as 640, 960 and 1536 pixel AVIF and WebP variants with JPEG fallbacks. Below-fold uses are lazy-loaded with explicit dimensions.

## Production approval checklist

- Confirm every visible handling and storage detail with a qualified safety reviewer.
- Confirm the imagery represents TGA's actual audience and services without implying facilities, instructors or events that do not exist.
- Obtain written TGA approval or replace with client-owned/licensed photography.
- Retain photographer/licensor, licence scope, model/property releases and expiry information for replacements.
- Recheck alt text, crops, responsive focal points and metadata after any replacement.
