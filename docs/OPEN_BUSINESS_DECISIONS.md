# Client decisions required

Sandbox defaults are illustrative, not TGA policy. Confirm membership categories, R450/R750 annual fees, joining fee (demo zero), manual versus automatic renewal, approval sequencing after payment, required document types (demo all optional), expiry requirements, certificate validity, assessment count/pass mark/attempt limits/time limits, accreditation (disabled), refund approvals/rules, legal/POPIA policy wording and retention, member migration, firearm sales/reservations, mobile scope and production providers. No SAPS integration or licence issuance is implemented.

Demo defaults: 12 month membership; portal-learning assessments; 80% pass threshold; three attempts; no time limit; certificates demo/non-accredited only. Seed and admin configuration control defaults; changing them does not require rewriting UI.
